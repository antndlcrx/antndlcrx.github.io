TITLE
LLM Agents and Evaluation | Tatiana Shavrina | Oxford LLMs 2026

DESCRIPTION
What does a benchmark score tell us about a language model or an AI agent?

In this lecture from the 2026 Oxford LLMs intensive, Tatiana Shavrina explores human preference, LLMs as judges, safety and societal impact, domain capabilities, and the evaluation of scientific research agents. She also examines benchmark contamination and the challenge of interpreting results that depend on the full evaluation setup.

- Safety and societal impact
